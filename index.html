Method

Each row is one project, rated on maturity and adoption on the date shown. Every claim carries a source link, and every dead verdict rests on dated evidence: an announced shutdown, a seized domain, or years without a commit and a site that no longer answers.

Tiers

S
In production or on mainnet for about two years or more, maintained in the last six months, and a category leader by measurable adoption or reference status.
A
Live in production or on mainnet with real, evidenced usage, maintained in the last six to twelve months.
B
Early mainnet, beta or limited usage, or a public testnet with active development in the last six months.
C
Research, spec, devnet, announced, hackathon stage, or dormant for twelve months or more with death unconfirmed.
D
The Graveyard: confirmed shut down, abandoned (site gone and quiet for 24 months or more), sanctioned or seized, or pivoted away from privacy. Every D row carries a source.
U
Unrated: listed with its facts and sources, without a tier, until the evidence is clear enough to rate.

Tiers measure maturity, not quality. They say how far a project has travelled and whether it is still moving. They make no claim about how good its privacy is.

Fields

Hides lists what the project keeps from observers. Trust names who you rely on: cryptography alone, a trusted operator, a committee or threshold, secure hardware, or a mix. Default says whether privacy is on for everyone or opt-in. Confidence grades how well the sources back the row.

Corrections

Projects and readers can claim or correct any row through the correction form. Bring a source and the row changes.
